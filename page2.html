Here you will find the building blocks of the overall development process for our autonomous rover. Our team will use these components to plan, track progress, and organize our work as the rover evolves from concept to final testing.

Product Requirements

The product requirements for our autonomous rover were developed to ensure the system could successfully complete all mission objectives while remaining practical, reliable, and safe to operate. The rover must be capable of acting autonomously, meaning it should navigate and make decisions without continuous human control. It must be an original design, including its mechanical structure, electrical system, and control software, developed by our team. The rover must be able to navigate to specified GPS coordinates, avoid obstacles, and travel across rough or sandy terrain while maintaining reasonable speed and positional accuracy. Additionally, it must be able to carry and deliver a 1.5-inch cubic wooden payload to designated locations.

The product requirements with weight percentage(%)

1. Autonomous GPS Navigation – 20%​
2. Positional Accuracy & Motion Performance –20%
3. Obstacle Detection & Avoidance – 15%​
4. Environmental & Terrain Durability – 15%
5. Fully Autonomous Operation – 10%​
6. Original integrated system design – 10%%​
7. Payload delivery capability 10%
*NOTE Weight Constraint – Required​ The rover shall have a total weight less than or equal to 2.5 lbs, including batteries and onboard electronics​
Gantt Chart

The team decided to use Google Calendar as there platform to use for the Gantt Chart The Gantt chart outlines the full timeline of our rover project, including design, prototyping, testing, and final validation phases. It helps ensure tasks are completed in a structured sequence while meeting project deadlines.

By organizing responsibilities and milestones visually, the team can track progress, adjust scheduling if necessary, and maintain accountability throughout the semester.
