Defining the Product

Here you will find our group’s stages when defining what we are going to build. Consider this to be us finding our footing on how exactly we plan to make our rover.

Product Requirements

The product requirements for our autonomous rover were developed to ensure the system could successfully complete all mission objectives while remaining practical, reliable, and safe to operate. The rover must be capable of acting autonomously, meaning it should navigate and make decisions without continuous human control. It must be an original design, including its mechanical structure, electrical system, and control software, developed by our team. The rover must be able to navigate to specified GPS coordinates, avoid obstacles, and travel across rough or sandy terrain while maintaining reasonable speed and positional accuracy. Additionally, it must be able to carry and deliver a 1.5-inch cubic wooden payload to designated locations. These requirements were chosen to align with the course objectives, real-world engineering constraints, and the challenges presented in the rover missions.

Engineering Specifications

Our engineering specifications convert our product requirements into measurable, testable targets. These quantitative standards define how the rover should perform in weight, speed, durability, accuracy, protection, obstacle detection, and autonomous navigation.

Engineering Specifications / Requirements

  • Commands must be able to be received by the rover, and the rover must execute the mission without inputs after mission start.
  • Rover mass (including payload) must be ≤ 2.5 lb.
  • Straight-line speed target of 1 ft/s.
  • Must endure a drop of 18 in without mission-critical damage.
  • Must be able to turn at approximately 20 degrees per second.
  • Distance travel accuracy must be within ±10% of the commanded value.
  • Must meet or exceed IP43 protection standards.
  • Ability to avoid obstacles as needed to achieve the mission goal.
  • Autonomously navigate to a received GPS coordinate.

More Quantified Standards

  • Total rover mass (including batteries and all onboard electronics) shall be ≤ 2.5 lb.
  • The rover shall achieve a straight-line ground speed of 1.0 ft/s ±10% on a flat surface.
  • The rover shall withstand a vertical drop of 18 inches onto a hard surface with no loss of functionality or mission-critical damage.
  • Commanded turning maneuvers (90° rotation) shall be completed within 5.0 s ± 0.5 s with an accuracy of ± 5°.
  • The rover shall travel commanded distances with an accuracy of ±10% of the commanded value for distances of 5 ft or greater.
  • The rover enclosure shall meet or exceed IP43 ingress protection standards.
  • The rover shall detect obstacles within 12 inches of its path and autonomously alter its trajectory to avoid collisions while continuing toward the mission goal.
  • The rover shall autonomously navigate to received GPS coordinates and stop within ± 5 ft of the target location.
